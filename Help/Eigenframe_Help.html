Eigenframe
Help and FAQ

Assign any static or dynamic scene to each of your Mission Control Spaces. Images, GIFs, and videos all supported.

In 1957, physicist Hugh Everett proposed that reality branches constantly into parallel coexisting universes, each one its own distinct and independent world. Eigenframe takes its name from that idea and applies it to your desktop. Each Space gets its own scene, still or moving, running quietly behind your work.
Version 1.0  ·  macOS 13+  ·  Apple Silicon

Installation

1
Open the DMG
Double-click Eigenframe.dmg. A Finder window opens showing the app and an Applications shortcut.
2
Drag to Applications
Drag the Eigenframe icon onto the Applications folder shortcut in the same window. Wait for the copy to complete.
3
Launch the app
Open Launchpad or Spotlight and launch Eigenframe. Because it is not from the App Store, macOS may warn you the first time. Right-click the app and choose Open to proceed. The app will not appear in the Dock as it lives in the menu bar.
4
Optional: enable Pause while typing
If you want video wallpapers to pause automatically while you type, turn on the Pause while typing switch at the bottom of the Eigenframe window. macOS will ask for permission, and Eigenframe will walk you through granting it. See Pause While Typing for full instructions. This feature is entirely optional and everything else works without it.
ℹ Eigenframe does not require Accessibility permission. Space detection uses CGSGetActiveSpace, a stable private system API that needs no special permission. Input Monitoring is only needed for the optional pause-while-typing feature.
◎

First Launch

1
Create your Spaces first
Swipe up with three fingers or press F3 to open Mission Control, then click the + button in the top-right corner to add Spaces.
2
Open the control panel
Click the ⊞ icon in your menu bar and choose Configure Spaces... The control panel opens showing a card for every Space you have created.
3
Assign scenes
Drag any supported image or video file onto a Space card, or right-click a card and choose a file from the picker. The card shows a thumbnail preview once a scene is assigned.
▶

Assigning Scenes

→
Drag and drop
Drag any supported image or video directly from Finder onto a Space card. The change takes effect immediately.
→
File picker
Right-click any Space card and choose Choose Scene... or Choose Different Scene... to open a standard file browser.
→
Remove a scene
Right-click a card that has a scene assigned and choose Remove Scene. That Space will revert to your standard macOS wallpaper.
✓ Assignments are saved automatically and restored every time the app launches. You only need to set them up once.

Pause While Typing

Eigenframe can pause your video wallpapers while you are typing and start them again when you stop. Some people find a moving background distracting while they work, and pausing also saves a little battery.

This feature is optional. Everything else in Eigenframe works whether or not you turn it on. If you skip this section, nothing else stops working.

🔒 What Eigenframe can and cannot see. To pause while you type, macOS has to tell Eigenframe that a key was pressed. Eigenframe is told only that some key went down. It is not told which key, and it never records, saves, or sends anything you type. macOS calls this Input Monitoring, and it asks your permission before allowing it.

Turning it on

1
Open the Eigenframe window
Click the Eigenframe icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen, then choose Open Control Panel. The menu bar is the strip along the very top of your screen where the clock and Wi-Fi icon live.
2
Turn on Pause while typing
At the bottom of the Eigenframe window there is a switch labelled Pause while typing. Click it so it turns on.
3
Follow the window that appears
Eigenframe opens a short walkthrough that takes you through the rest one step at a time. It watches for the permission being granted and moves itself along when that happens, and you can step back at any point. Click Open System Settings on the first step.
4
Switch Eigenframe on in the list
System Settings opens to a list of apps. Find Eigenframe and click the switch next to its name so it turns blue. Your Mac may ask for your password, Touch ID, or Apple Watch first. That is normal, and it is your Mac asking, not Eigenframe.

If Eigenframe is not in the list, click the small + button at the bottom left of the list, choose Eigenframe from your Applications folder, and click Open. It will then appear in the list with a switch you can turn on.
5
Quit Eigenframe and open it again
This step is easy to miss and the feature will not work without it. macOS only applies a new permission when an app starts fresh. Click the Eigenframe icon in the menu bar and choose Quit, then open Eigenframe again from Applications or Spotlight.
⚠ Closing the window is not the same as quitting. Eigenframe has no icon in the Dock because it lives in the menu bar. If you click the red button to close the Eigenframe window, the app keeps running. To actually quit it, click the Eigenframe icon in the menu bar and choose Quit.

If it still does not work

•
Eigenframe is already switched on, but typing does nothing
Switch it off, wait a moment, then switch it on again. When an app is replaced with a newer version, macOS sometimes keeps showing the old setting as on while no longer honoring it. Turning it off and on again clears that up. Quit Eigenframe from the menu bar afterwards and open it again.
•
Eigenframe is not in the list at all
Apps usually appear in that list only after they have asked for the permission, so an empty entry is normal at first. You can add Eigenframe yourself. At the bottom left of the list there is a small + button, next to a − button. Click +, find Eigenframe in your Applications folder, select it, and click Open. Eigenframe then appears in the list with a switch beside it, which you can turn on.

You can also prompt Eigenframe to ask on its own by turning the Pause while typing switch off and then on again inside the app.
•
You cannot find the permission list
Open the Apple menu at the top left of your screen, choose System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, then Input Monitoring. Or let Eigenframe do it for you with the button in the window it shows.
•
You would rather not grant the permission
That is a reasonable choice. Turn the Pause while typing switch off and Eigenframe will stop asking. Every other feature keeps working exactly as before.
ℹ If you built Eigenframe yourself from the source code rather than installing the released app, the permission may not stick between builds unless you sign the app with a stable certificate. See the README for how to set that up. This does not affect anyone using the downloaded release.

Performance

Short clips under a few minutes at 1080p or lower will give the smoothest experience regardless of format. Very large files, high resolutions, or multiple simultaneous videos may increase GPU and CPU usage, affect battery life, and cause delayed startup or occasional dropped frames.

⚠
If you notice performance issues, consider re-encoding your video to a shorter, more compressed version before assigning it. See the Re-encoding section below for free tools and commands.
→
Resolution matters most
A 4K video uses far more GPU resources than a 1080p video of the same length. If your source is 4K, scaling it down before assigning it makes a significant difference.
→
Codec matters too
H.264 and HEVC are hardware decoded on M1 and later chips, using minimal resources. Other codecs may fall back to software decoding which uses more CPU.
→
Multiple Spaces multiply the load
Each Space with a video assigned runs its own decode process. Four Spaces with four videos is four simultaneous operations. On laptops, consider using Pause from the menu bar during intensive work.

Re-encoding Videos

If your video is large, slow to load, or causing performance issues, re-encoding it before assigning it makes a significant difference. Two free tools make this straightforward.

→
HandBrake (easiest)
Free, open source, drag and drop. Download from handbrake.fr. Use the preset H.264 MKV 1080p30 or H.265 MKV 1080p30 for the best results. Enable the Audio tab and remove all audio tracks before encoding.
→
ffmpeg (command line)
Install via Homebrew: brew install ffmpeg. Then use any of the commands below. The -an flag removes audio in all cases, keeping file sizes smaller since Eigenframe mutes all wallpapers anyway.

Re-encode to H.264 1080p:

ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -vf scale=1920:1080 -an output.mp4

Re-encode to HEVC for smaller file size:

ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx265 -crf 28 -an output.mp4

Trim to 30 seconds and compress:

ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -t 30 -an output.mp4

Scale down 4K to 1080p:

ffmpeg -i input_4k.mp4 -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -vf scale=1920:1080 -an output.mp4

Remove audio only, keep original quality:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -c:v copy -an output.mp4
ℹ A lower -crf value means higher quality and larger file size. 18 is near-lossless. 28 is efficient with small visible quality reduction. For wallpaper use, 23 to 28 is usually indistinguishable from the original at normal viewing distance.

Known Issues

⚠
Window level after sleep - The wallpaper window may reappear on top of other windows after the display wakes from sleep. Quitting and relaunching fixes this.
⚠
New Spaces added at runtime - Spaces created after the app launches are not automatically detected. Click Refresh Spaces in the control panel after adding a new Space.
⚠
Gatekeeper warning on first launch. Because Eigenframe is not distributed through the Mac App Store, macOS will show a security warning the first time you open it. Right-click the app and choose Open, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
⚠
Wallpaper transition delay. There is a brief delay when switching Spaces before the wallpaper appears. This is a macOS compositor constraint. Using short, hardware-decoded video files (H.264 or HEVC) reduces the delay.
⚠
Input Monitoring permission after rebuilding. If you build from source and use ad-hoc signing, the Input Monitoring permission will not persist across rebuilds. Use a self-signed certificate named Eigenframe Dev as described in the README to keep the permission stable.
