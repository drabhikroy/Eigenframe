# Example Media Credits

These files are included to help you get started with Eigenframe. Both are NASA/ESA public domain imagery and may be used freely with attribution.

## black_hole_accretion_disk.png

**Title:** Black Hole Accretion Disk Visualization  
**Credit:** NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center / Jeremy Schnittman  
**Source:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14146/  
**Description:** A visualization of a black hole illustrating how its gravity distorts our view, warping its surroundings as if seen in a carnival mirror. The simulation shows infalling matter collected into a thin, hot accretion disk.

## cosmic_tornado_HH49_50.mp4

**Title:** Flow through Cosmic Tornado — Herbig-Haro 49/50  
**Credit:** ESA/Webb, NASA, CSA, STScI  
**Source:** https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Videos/2025/03/Flow_through_Cosmic_Tornado_Herbig-Haro_49_50  
**Description:** A James Webb Space Telescope visualization flying through the Herbig-Haro 49/50 protostellar outflow, located approximately 630 light-years from Earth in the constellation Chamaeleon.
